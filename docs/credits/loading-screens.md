# Loading screen photo credits

Some Freedom Arrow loading screens are AI re-paintings built around real photographs from Wikimedia Commons. The photographers and licences:

- **AbramsX** (FA_US_LOADING_11_abramsx): "AbramsX.jpg" by Abovfold, CC BY 4.0.
- **T-14 Armata** (FA_RU_LOADING_12_t14_armata): "T-14 Armata (27053434067).jpg" by Dmitriy Fomin, CC BY 2.0.
- **T-15 Armata** (FA_RU_LOADING_13_t15_armata_ifv): built around a reference photo supplied by the team (19 Nov 2026); photographer not yet identified.
- **Su-47 Berkut** (FA_RU_LOADING_11_su47_berkut): "Sukhoi Su-47 in formation, 2005.jpg" by Sergey Krivchikov, GFDL 1.2.
- **F-35A** (FA_US_LOADING_12_f35a): newly generated; USAF public-domain photos and the F-35A three-view drawing on Commons were used as shape references only.

- **Object 640 Black Eagle** (FA_RU_LOADING_14_black_eagle): built around a reference image supplied by the team (19 Nov 2026); photographer not identified.
- **Su-75 Checkmate** (FA_RU_LOADING_15_su75_checkmate): "Sukhoi S-75 Checkmate mockup side at Dubai Air Show 2021.jpg" by Mztourist, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **T-80BVM** (FA_RU_LOADING_16_t80bvm): "T-80BVM MBT Army-2022 2022-08-20 2400.jpg" by Mike1979 Russia, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **XM30 and M10 Booker** (FA_US_LOADING_13_xm30_m10): built around a reference image supplied by the team (an American Rheinmetall XM30 render); source not identified.
- **B-21 Raider** (FA_US_LOADING_14_b21_raider): built around a reference image supplied by the team (the U.S. Air Force B-21 artist rendering).
- **YF-23 and F-22A** (FA_US_LOADING_15_yf23_f22): built around reference photos supplied by the team; the F-22A was redrawn using U.S. Air Force public-domain photos ("F-22 Raptor edit1.jpg", "Lockheed Martin F-22 Raptor in flight, starboard side.jpg") as shape references.
- **M10 Booker** (FA_US_LOADING_16_m10_booker): "M10 Booker.jpg" by the United States Army, public domain.

Each photo-based screen also shows the photographer's name in the bottom-left corner (Osama, 19 Nov 2026); the full credit and licence are here.

The images derived from the GFDL 1.2 photo (Su-47) and the CC BY-SA 4.0 photos (Su-75, T-80BVM) are offered under the same licences as their sources.
