## ⚠️ Models that look like game extracts (Osama decides)

This is evidence from file and object names only. Nothing was removed.

**Strong signs of War Thunder extraction (4):**
- `AH-64E.zip`: one `ah_64e_china_assembled.blend` (268 MB) and textures such as `us_gauge_altimeter_ah64d_c.png`, `pilot_us_500_d.png`, `aircraft_normal_detail_n.png`, `glass_dirt.png`, `ah_64e_netherlands_n.png`.
- `F-16A.zip`: `f_16a_block_20_mlu_assembled.blend` (528 MB), `pilot_it_late_500_d.png`, `uk_gauge_clock_type_a_13a_c.png`, `golden_coating.png`.
- `Mirage 2000.zip`: `mirage_2000_5ei_assembled.blend` (430 MB), `fr_gauge_*` cockpit gauges, `damocles_pod_c.png`.
- `M60A3 TTS.zip`: `cn_m60a3_tts_assembled.blend` (164 MB), `bag_roll_d_c.png`, `gas_jerrycan_a_c.png`.
- Why: `*_assembled.blend`, the `_c`/`_n` suffixes, `pilot_*_500_d` and the `cn_` prefix match War Thunder file naming. These 4 are also exactly the Taiwan vehicles in War Thunder's China tree.

**Strong sign of GTA V extraction (2 bodies, one listing):**
- `ROC Marine.zip` and `ROC Marine SF.zip` (cgmodel listing 749770): the meshes are named `s_m_y_marine_03_model`, GTA V's US Marine ped. The textures follow GTA V ped naming: `uppr_diff_000_a_uni`, `lowr_diff_000_a_uni`, `head_diff_000_a_whi`, `accs_diff_000_a_uni`.
- These bodies are used by 5 squads: Marine, Marine AT, Reserve marine, Marine Frogmen and Marine Frogmen CQC.

**Weak signs (source unclear, ask Shawn):**
- `ROC Infantry 2.zip`: mesh `JGSF_soldier_01`, textures `japan_gunner_heads_*` and `japan_gunner_late_*`.
- `ROC Infantry.zip` (cgmodel 781683): `SM_PMC_shoes_01`. On its own this proves nothing.
- `CM-12.zip` (cgmodel 680292): textures `body_c`, `barrel_n`, `browning_ao`, `_s`. This is a common naming style too.

## 🧩 Missing, wrong or unusable files

- **Chung Hsiang II UAV:** the sheet names `Chung Xiang II.zip`; the folder has no such file.
- **CM-12:** `CM-12.zip` contains a model named CM-11 (`CM11.fbx`, objects `CM11_body`, `CM11_turret`, `CM11_m60` …). The real CM-12 model is missing, or this is a duplicate CM-11.
- **ROC SF2:** the sheet names `ROC SF2.zip` for SF Scout and SF Section; the folder has `ROC SF 1.zip` instead. Confirm it is the same file.
- **Hsiung Feng IIE:** Unreal Engine `.uasset` files only. We need an FBX/OBJ export.
- **UH-60M:** the file is a Taiwan S-70C (`台湾s70c`), not a UH-60M, at 666,927 triangles. That is about 13 times a vanilla helicopter (AH-64E LOD0 51.9k).
- **Not in the sheet:** `FBX_TK2222.zip` (two meshes `M1`/`M2`, 0.25 m: a helmet, the same `TK_M1` textures as `ROC Infantry 2`). Tell us which squad uses it.
- **Card files:** SF Scout and SF Section have pictures only, no JSON. SF Snipers and SF Snipers M107 have neither; the sheet refers to an "Infantry Stats" tab and two screenshots that are not shared.

## 🧾 Card data checks

- **Faction:** every card uses the US flag (country 2). ROC fits as a US specialization, like Blue Dragon, with no new country. Osama decides.
- **DLC templates:** 4 cards copy DLC units. CM-34 uses CV-9035 (DLC), Land Sword II uses NASAMS (DLC2), M109A5 uses M109A5Ö (DLC2), and the DMS Team card is built on RBS 70 (unit 524, DLC2). Players without the DLC may not get them. Non-DLC choices: Stryker ICV or Bradley, SLAMRAAM, M109A6 Paladin, Stinger team (69).
- **M1A2T:** the sheet says SEP v3 (no APS); the card file is built on M1A2 SEP v2 (unit 243). Pick one.
- **Kestrel-1:** the real Kestrel is an unguided rocket launcher, but the cards make it an anti-tank missile with the Spike SR icon. Its values differ between cards: 8.5 damage / 450 mm / 10-12 s on Marine and Marine AT, 6.5 / 300 mm / 18 s on Reserver Kestrel.
- **T74:** the Reserve marine card fires 5.56 mm from the T74; the T112 card gives it 7.62 mm. The T74 is a 7.62 mm machine gun.
- **Hit points:** the `health` field differs from the hit-point stat on 4 cards (Reserve marine 65 vs 50, Reserver and Reserver Kestrel 65 vs 45, M1A2T 17 vs 14). The stat value will be used unless Shawn says otherwise.
- **Unfinished weapon values:** M72A4 LAW (Mech. Riflemen), M107 (Marine Frogmen), T74 and Kestrel-1 (T112) have no range, damage or reload on the card.
- **CM-26:** the sheet marks it unverified. The model is a small tracked vehicle, 8.0k triangles. Tell us its role and weapons.
- **Card art:** the 10 artworks are 2752 × 1536 and usable. 4 carry the "BROKEN ARROW" logo and fine print bottom-left; these would be removed, as on the community PLA art.
