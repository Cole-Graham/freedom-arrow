## NOTES ON DATA

1. **Bomb Category Range Classification**:
   - **Dumb gravity bombs**: 2000-3000m (ballistic drop trajectory)
   - **Retarded fuze bombs**: 2000m (slowed descent, safer delivery)
   - **Guided JDAM variants**: 30000m (GPS/laser guided standoff)
   - **Extended-range glide**: 40000m (Stormbreaker GPS-guided, longer standoff)
   - **Cluster munitions**: 2000-3000m (area weapons, ballistic)

2. **Platform Variants**: Same bomb maintains consistent range across platforms
   - Mk82: 3000m standard, 2000m retarded (Snakeye)
   - FAB variants: 3000m standard, 2000m retarded
   - GBU JDAM: 30000m all variants (54/55/56)
   - KAB guided: 30000m all variants (50/500/1500)

3. **Guidance System Impact**:
   - Gravity bombs: ballistic 2000-3000m
   - Laser/TV guided (KAB, LJDAM): 30000m standoff
   - GPS/INS guided (GBU-57): 30000m standoff
   - Extended GPS glide (GBU-53): 40000m standoff
   - Unguided cluster: 2000-3000m ballistic

4. **Special Ordnance**:
   - **Thermobaric** (ODAB): 3000m (gravity fuel-air)
   - **Napalm** (Mk77, ZB-500): 2000m (incendiary)
   - **Bunker Buster** (GBU-57): 30000m (hardened penetrator)
   - **Cluster-guided** (PBK-500U): 30000m (TV-guided cluster)
