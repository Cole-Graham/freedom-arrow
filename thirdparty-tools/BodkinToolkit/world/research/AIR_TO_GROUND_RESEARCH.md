## NOTES ON DATA

1. **Conservative Effective Range Approach**: Used practical combat ranges
   - Short-range (5-15km): tactical air-to-ground (Griffin, Kh-25, Kh-29, Kh-38, Kh-BPLA)
   - Medium-range (20-40km): semi-active/passive seeker missiles (Sidearm, Maverick, HARM, Kh-31, Kh-58)
   - Long-range (100-160km): cruise missiles (SLAM-ER, JSM, Kh-32, Kh-35, Kh-59)
   - Glide munitions (25km): JSOW variants

2. **Platform Variants**: Same missile maintains consistent range across platforms
   - Sidearm: 20km (aircraft and helicopter)
   - Griffin: 12km (all platforms: MC-130H, AC-130, MQ-1, MQ-1C)
   - Kh-31: 40km (aircraft and helicopter)
   - Kh-35U: 160km (aircraft and helicopter)
   - Kh-59MK: 160km (all variants)

3. **Missile System Classification**:
   - **Anti-radiation (ARM)**: Sidearm (20km), HARM (35km), Kh-31 (40km), Kh-58UShK (35km)
   - **Anti-ship cruise**: SLAM-ER (150km), Kh-32 (160km), Kh-35U (160km), Kh-59MK (160km)
   - **Air-to-ground guided**: Maverick (25km), Griffin (12km), Kh-25ML (10km), Kh-29 (15km), Kh-38M (10km)
   - **Cruise variants**: JSM (100km), SLAM-ER (150km), Kh-59MK (160km)
   - **Glide munitions**: JSOW (25km)
   - **UAV-launched**: Kh-BPLA (5km)

4. **Engagement Type Differences**:
   - Standard air-to-ground: 10-25km (tactical engagement)
   - Anti-radiation (ARM): 20-40km (standoff from radar threat)
   - Anti-ship/cruise: 100-160km (long-range standoff)

5. **System Characteristics**:
   - **Sidearm**: Passive seeker anti-radiation, 20km range
   - **HARM**: Active radar seeker, 35km+ range
   - **Maverick**: Electro-optical, 25km range (H variant)
   - **Griffin**: Precision small guided missile, 12km range
   - **JSOW**: Glide munition, 20-25km from altitude
   - **Cruise missiles** (SLAM-ER, JSM, Kh-59, Kh-32, Kh-35): 100-160km range
