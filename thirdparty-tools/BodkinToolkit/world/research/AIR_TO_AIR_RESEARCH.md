## NOTES ON DATA

1. **Engagement Range Classification**:
   - **WVR (Within Visual Range)**: 10-15km (short-range IR missiles: AIM-9, R-60, R-73)
   - **Medium-range BVR**: 25-40km (semi-active/thermal: AIM-7M, R-27 family)
   - **Extended BVR**: 80-100km (active radar: AMRAAM, R-77)
   - **Long-range**: 100-120km (advanced active radar: R-37, AMRAAM-ER, AIM-260)

2. **Platform Variants**: Same missile maintains consistent effective range
   - AIM-9: 15km (aircraft, helicopter, ground-launched SAM)
   - AMRAAM: 100km (aircraft and ground-launched)
   - R-27 variants: 25-40km (aircraft platforms)
   - R-60/R-73: 10km (aircraft and helicopter)

3. **Guidance System Impact**:
   - **Infrared/WVR**: 10-15km (passive, short-range: AIM-9, R-60, R-73)
   - **Semi-active Radar**: 25-40km (datalink required: AIM-7M, R-27)
   - **Active Radar**: 80-120km (autonomous seeker: AMRAAM, R-77, R-37, AIM-260)
   - **Extended variants**: +20-30km over baseline

4. **Missile System Characteristics**:
   - **AIM-9 Sidewinder**: Infrared WVR, 15km, early BVR role
   - **AIM-7M Sparrow**: Semi-active radar, 35km, pre-AMRAAM BVR
   - **AIM-120 AMRAAM**: Active radar, 100km, modern standard
   - **AIM-174**: Long-range active radar, 40km practical
   - **AIM-260**: Next-generation long-range, 120km+ advanced
   - **R-60**: Short-range infrared, 10km, WVR tactical
   - **R-73**: Short-range infrared, 10km, modern WVR
   - **R-27**: Semi-active family, 25-40km depending on variant
   - **R-37**: Long-range active radar, 100km strategic
   - **R-77**: Medium-range active radar, 80km modern BVR

5. **Engagement Domain Note**:
   - Air-to-air missiles use **low_alt** (helicopters <100m) and **high_alt** (aircraft 100-1500m) domains
   - Ground domain is 0 for air-to-air missiles (no surface engagement)
