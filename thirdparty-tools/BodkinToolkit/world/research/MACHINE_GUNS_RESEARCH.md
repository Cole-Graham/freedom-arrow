## NOTES ON DATA

1. **Conservative Approach**: Where military sources diverged, shorter/more conservative range was used
   - Example: M240 shows 800-1800m; used 1200m for sustained fire capability
   
2. **Aircraft Miniguns**: Rotary-gun helicopter systems optimized for suppression/area fire; effective ranges similar to vehicle-mounted equivalents (~1500m)
   
3. **Suppressed Variants**: PBS suppressors maintain base rifle/gun effective range (no reduction for combat capability)
   
4. **CQC/Variant Designations**: Specialized variants (CQC, T1-T3, bullpup) use base platform effective ranges per military standard
   
5. **Soviet 7.62x54mm Standard**: PKT/PKP/PKM all standardized to 1500m effective range per military specifications
   
6. **NATO 7.62x51mm Standard**: M240/MG3/FNMAG variants use 1200m for GPMG role (800-1800m range, conservative sustained fire estimate)
