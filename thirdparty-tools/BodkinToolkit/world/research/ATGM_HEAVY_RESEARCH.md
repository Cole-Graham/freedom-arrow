## NOTES ON DATA

1. **Conservative Approach**: Where sources diverged, shorter/more conservative ranges were used
   - Example: Kornet listed as 5-5.5km; used 5500m for infantry/vehicle variants
   - Example: LMUR listed as 14.5-15km; used 14500m as published figure
   - Example: JAGM variants show 8-16km; used 10000m as baseline effective range

2. **Platform Variants**: Same missile maintains unified effective range across platforms
   - Kornet (all variants): 5500m (man-portable to heavy vehicle)
   - Ataka-M (all variants): 8000m (helicopter, UAV, tracked auto-loader)
   - Vikhr (all variants): 10000m (helicopter, UAV, fixed-wing)
   - Hellfire (all variants): 8000m except JAGM (10000m upgraded)

3. **Generational Progression**: Later generation systems show extended ranges
   - Kokon: 5000m
   - Khrizantema: 6000m
   - Kornet: 5500m → Kornet-M: 8000m (30% range increase)
   - Hellfire: 8000m → JAGM: 10000m (25% range increase)

4. **Warhead Impact**: Thermobaric variants maintain effective range of HEAT equivalents
   - No range reduction for thermobaric ordnance
   - Same guidance system, same flight envelope

5. **Platform Categories**:
   - **Vehicle-Mounted**: Kokon, Khrizantema, Kornet (tracked auto-loader)
   - **Helicopter-Mounted**: Ataka-M, Kornet, Vikhr, Hermes-A, LMUR
   - **UAV-Capable**: Kornet, Ataka-M, Vikhr, Hellfire
   - **Fixed-Wing**: Vikhr (Su-25T), Hellfire (various), LMUR (Mi-28NM/Ka-52M)

6. **Extreme Range Systems**:
   - Hermes-A: 15000m (specialized anti-armor from Ka-52K Katran)
   - LMUR: 14500m (light multirole from modern Mi-28NM/Ka-52M)
   - Both representing next-generation standoff capability
