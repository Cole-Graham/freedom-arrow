## NOTES ON DATA

1. **Short-Range Classification (Category 21)**:
   - VSHORAD (very short): 5-10km (Strela-10, RBS 70, SeaRAM)
   - SHORAD (short): 10-30km (Tunguska, Osa, Pantsir, Sosna, Tor)
   - Engagement altitude: low (primarily helicopters, low-flying aircraft)

2. **Medium-Range Classification (Category 22)**:
   - Tactical medium: 40-50km (BUK, IRIS-T, ESSM)
   - Engagement altitude: medium (tactical aircraft, cruise missiles)
   - Mobile platforms

3. **Long-Range Classification (Category 23)**:
   - Strategic long-range: 75-100km (S-300/S-400, Patriot)
   - Extended naval: 240km (SM-6 naval SAM)
   - Engagement altitude: high (strategic aircraft, cruise missiles)
   - Mobile and fixed platforms

4. **System Characteristics**:
   - **Short-range**: Primarily anti-helicopter, close-in defense, tactical
   - **Medium-range**: Regional air defense, mobile systems
   - **Long-range**: Strategic territorial defense, fixed/mobile

5. **Platform Notes**:
   - Ground-based SAMs operate in low_alt and high_alt domains (not ground)
   - Ranges represent effective intercept ranges for target altitudes
