## NOTES ON DATA

1. **Conservative Approach**: Where sources diverged, shorter/more conservative ranges were used
   - Example: AT4 listed as 300m effective (reported as 300m standard, up to 600m extended range)
   - Example: SRAW: 200m moving targets vs. 600m stationary; used 600m as maximum effective

2. **Disposable vs. Reusable**: Disposable LAW/AT4 systems optimized for immediate effect vs. Carl Gustaf crew-served for sustained fire
   - LAW/AT4: Single-shot disposable, optimized for surprise/mobility
   - Carl Gustaf: Crew weapon, optimized for flexible engagement across ranges

3. **Thermobaric Systems**: Lower ranges than kinetic anti-tank systems due to blast/pressure damage vs. penetration
   - RPO-A/RPOM: 200-300m blast-effective radius
   - RShG-1: 150m (smallest thermobaric system)
   - CATFAE: Large vehicle-mounted system, extended range capability

4. **Vehicle-Mounted vs. Infantry**: Same ammunition fired from vehicle platforms may vary slightly in practical range
   - SPG-9/2A28: 700-800m direct fire (HE/HEAT distinction)
   - MLRS A-22: Naval system adapted to land vehicles, very long strategic range (4500m+)

5. **RPG Variants**: Multiple generations with different effective ranges
   - RPG-7 series: 500m effective (limited by unguided ballistics)
   - RPG-16: 800m (improved rocket motor, better accuracy)
   - RPG-26: 250m (disposable variant, shorter range)
