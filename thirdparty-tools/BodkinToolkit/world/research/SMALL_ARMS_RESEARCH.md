## NOTES ON DATA

1. **Conservative Approach**: Where military sources diverged, shorter/more conservative range was used (e.g., SVD 600-800m → used 800m for area target capability)
2. **No Anti-Air Capability**: All small arms entries have `low_alt: 0` and `high_alt: 0` (not applicable)
3. **Suppressor Impact**: Suppression adds stealth/noise reduction but does NOT reduce effective combat range on same caliber/barrel
4. **Ammunition Variants**: T-5000 uses .338 Lapua (1500m effective); M2010 uses .300 WM (1200m effective)
5. **Optics & Configuration**: RPK-74M/M249 values use optical sight variants (500m/800m respectively); iron sights would be shorter
6. **CQB Variants**: Mk18 CQBR (300m) and suppressed rifles maintain base platform effective range but are optimized for close range deployment
