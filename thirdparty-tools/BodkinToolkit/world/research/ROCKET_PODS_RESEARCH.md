## NOTES ON DATA

1. **Conservative Effective Range Approach**: Used practical combat ranges where sources differed
   - Zuni 127mm: 8km published effective range
   - S-13 122mm: 6km standard (practical 1-3km, max 6km used)
   - S-25 340mm: 5km conservative (heavy rocket, lower velocity)
   - Hydra 70mm: 7km standard unguided
   - Hydra APKWS/DAGR: 8km guided (precision extends range)
   - Fletcher: 3km man-portable (limited range)
   - S-8 80mm: 4.5km (lighter Soviet rocket)

2. **Platform Variants**: Same rocket system maintains consistent effective range
   - Zuni: 8000m across helicopter and aircraft variants
   - S-13: 6000m across helicopter and aircraft variants
   - S-25: 5000m (aircraft only)
   - Standard Hydra: 7000m (helicopter and aircraft)
   - APKWS: 8000m (guided precision)
   - Fletcher: 3000m (man-portable/infantry)
   - DAGR: 8000m (guided precision)
   - S-8: 4500m (helicopter and aircraft)

3. **Guidance System Impact**: Guided variants extend range beyond ballistic envelope
   - Standard unguided: ballistic trajectory limits (4-7km)
   - APKWS guided: precision guidance adds ~1-2km (8km total)
   - DAGR guided: precision guidance adds ~1-2km (8km total)
   - Fletcher: man-portable constraint reduces range (3km)

4. **Launch Platform Variants**: Different platforms use same rockets with similar range
   - Helicopter variants: same ballistic envelope as aircraft
   - Ground variants (LAVAD, LAV-AD): same rocket, different platform
   - Man-portable variants: same rocket family, reduced range

5. **Rocket System Family Progression**:
   - Small diameter (70mm FFAR/Hydra): 7000m standard, 8000m guided
   - Medium diameter (80-122mm): 4500-6000m unguided
   - Large diameter (127-340mm): 5000-8000m (127mm higher velocity, 340mm lower)
   - Guided variants: +1000-2000m over ballistic base
